How the prediction works

  1. History: each team starts at its Statbotics EPA entering the event or, if that's unavailable, its points per match (OPR) at its earlier events this season. Win/loss record adds a small edge.
  2. Today's matches: every completed match at the event updates the ratings. The latest day of play counts the most.
  3. Your bias: the sliders nudge teams up or down.
  4. Jev AI: when enabled, Jev reads all of this and picks a winner and score band. Its answer is blended with the stats model.
  5. Auto-tuning: once a day the model replays recent finished events and keeps the settings with the smallest score miss.
  6. Limits: scores swing from match to match (breakdowns, penalties, luck). Even in hindsight a typical alliance score is off by about 18% of the average, so ±5 points is rare.
